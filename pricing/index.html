Why the price is per category

An AI answer engine does not rank a company. It answers a question. "Best boutique hotel in Waikiki" and "where to stay near the convention center" are different questions with different winners, and a business can be named in one and absent from the other.

A category is the set of questions a real buyer would ask to end up choosing someone like you. We fix that set at the start, then measure the same questions every run, so a change in the results is a change in the world rather than a change in what we asked. An agency running four client categories pays for four. See the reseller math.

The questions people actually ask

Can I cancel?

Monitor is month to month. Cancel whenever you like and it stops at the end of the period you have paid for. There is no notice period and no exit fee. The Audit baseline month is the one exception, because it is the run that everything afterward is compared against. Full terms are on the terms page.

Why binary instead of a percentage?

Because a percentage from a small sample looks precise and is not. Monitor runs enough to tell you reliably whether you were named, and reporting that as "cited in 62% of queries" would dress up a thin sample as a measurement.

Audit runs three full passes a month, which is what it takes to say something quantitative and defend it. That difference is the honest reason the two tiers are priced apart.

Do you touch my website?

No, and this is not a limitation we are apologizing for. We measure and we hand you the list. Your team or your agency executes it. A vendor who does the work and then grades the work is not a measurement.

What does the 90-day credit mean?

If you start on Monitor and move up to Audit within 90 days, everything you paid on Monitor comes off the $950 baseline. Starting small costs you nothing if you later decide you need the full instrument.

Will you promise my citations go up?

No. We measure what the engines cite. We do not claim that any particular change causes an engine to cite you, because we have not proven that and neither has anyone else selling it. What you get is an accurate picture and a prioritized list, on a schedule.

Who is behind it?

You work with the principal. There is no account team between you and the person running the measurement. The full methodology is published, including the engines, the query-set discipline, and how sources get classified.
